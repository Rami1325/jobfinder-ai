### A list on a phone, the board from `md` (PLAN 31.2/7, 2026-09-23)

- **What it was, measured at 390x664.** Five counters and two rate rings in one card filled the first screen, and the board's first column started at **y = 609 of 664**. The five status columns were swiped sideways, one per screen, and each card showed its status twice: a split-flap chip and a full-width `<select>` under it.
- **Below `md` it is status tabs over one vertical list** (the list at **y = 290**). Each tab carries its count, and the page opens on the first status that has anything in it, so it never lands on an empty tab while another holds the user's applications. A status change moves the card to its new tab, and C2's accent flash plays on the tab that received it. From `md` the five-column grid is unchanged.
- **Only ONE of the two is mounted** (`useBoardFits`, a live `matchMedia` on 768 px), never both behind CSS. Both render every card, and the board's `layoutId` glide and the split-flap's `pendingFlips` entry each assume one copy of a card on the page: a second copy would register the same `layoutId` and consume the flip the visible one needed.
- **The status is ONE tappable chip**: the split-flap face shows it, a chevron says it opens, and a native `<select>` lies over it, transparent, labelled "Status". A phone opens its own picker and a screen reader hears "Status, Applied". The full-width select is gone, on the board as well.
- **The list's sort includes the date applied** (`lib/trackerSort.ts`): Newest (the day it was added), Date applied, and Match. Under Date applied every row WITH an applied date comes before every row without one, newest first on both sides: an unknown date is not an early one (the house rule), and the explicit rule holds even if the direction is ever flipped. People rely on this sort; LinkedIn's new tracker drew complaints for dropping it, some from people who report their applications for unemployment.
- **The counters are one line** ("3 Total · 1 Applied · 0 Interviews · 0 Offers · 0 Declined"), and **the two rings moved to Analytics**, at its top, with the denominator still riding along as the label ("100%" over one application reads as that). The "gone quiet" nudge comes first, above the counters.
- check-mirrors 58 EXECUTES the sort (undated after dated, by score, by the day added, no sorting in place) and pins the one mount, the labelled chip, no full-width select, and the rings on Analytics rather than the board. Measured at 390x664 in both languages and at 1440x900.

### One job, one page (PLAN 31.4/2, 2026-09-23)

- **A tracked job has a page of its own, `/applications/:id` (`pages/JobPage.tsx`), and it replaced the detail modal.** It is built from the row and the emails the inbox tied to it (`data-and-privacy.md` has what the row keeps): the header (title, company · place · "Posted …", the status as one tappable chip, the stars, the Match chip only when scored, Open the posting), What they ask for (the stored analysis's must-haves and nice-to-haves, or a sentence saying how to get one, and the posting one tap away), your resume for the job (the draft's REAL first page from `usePageImages`, Word and PDF in the template it was saved in, the way back to it on the document while this tab still holds its review, or Tailor with the fit check's price under it, or a batch draft on its way), the letter (the tailor page's own `CoverLetter`, keyed by the stored analysis so a pass the tailor page opened still covers it; with no analysis stored, "Write a cover letter" reads the posting first, a daily count and never a monthly use, stores it on the row, then shows the card with its price), Prepare (Interview questions, Mock interview, Company brief, Outreach, Follow-up, by their own names, each with `?app=<id>`), the timeline and the notes. From `lg` the applications are a list beside it, mounted only when shown, so a phone never fetches it. **Viewing it never calls a model**: no effect on the page calls a model route (check-mirrors 65), so a model call only follows a tap on a control that states its cost.
- **Prepare opens each tool with the job, and Back returns to it** (PLAN 31.4/3). Every tool's Back said "All tools", and the posting a tool was opened with rode navigation state, gone after a reload. Now the job page links Interview questions, Mock interview (`&mode=mock`), Company brief, Outreach and Follow-up with `?app=<id>`, and each tool reads the job through `hooks/useJobContext` (one `GET /applications/{id}`; an unreadable job leaves the tool empty and Back still leads to its page, which says what happened). A tool fills ONLY the fields still empty (`set…((cur) => cur || job.…)`), so what the user typed, or a caller's state, wins; the follow-up writer's stage moves to the one the job's status calls for only while it is still the untouched default. `ToolShell` takes `back` ("Back to the job"; the tools list when no job was named), Interview renders its own link, and Interview's Company brief button carries the job on. Driven at 390 in both languages: all five open filled in, stay filled through a reload, and return to the job. check-mirrors 67.
- **The timeline says only what the row records**: when it was added (or "Added from your email"), when it was sent (`applied_at`), and the ONE status change a row keeps, when a person made it past Applied; every change an email made is in the emails under it, with its Undo. There is no history table, so nothing is reconstructed.
- **A card IS the way to its page.** Its title is a link stretched over the card (`after:absolute after:inset-0`), which stays inside the card only while both card wrappers are `relative`, and the stars, the status chip and the Interviewed toggle sit above it (`relative z-10`), so they act in place. The row of three buttons under every card (the posting, the modal's eye, Delete) is gone: all three are on the job's page. The notes chip is a plain indicator now; a tap on it is a tap on the card.
- **Delete on the job's page runs HERE**, through `remove` and its undo window (check-mirrors 51): the page navigates to `/tracker` with `{ remove: id }`, and the tracker waits for its list, clears that navigation state FIRST (so Back or a reload never deletes a second time) and then removes the card with its Undo toast. Driven at 390: the Interview tab went 1 → 0 → 1 through Delete and Undo with the row kept on the server, and a delete left alone reached the server only after the window.
- **Two bidi traps met on the page, both fixed**: the meta line's dots sit OUTSIDE its `dir="auto"` parts (inside an English part a dot landed on its far end under the Hebrew UI, "· Tel Aviv, IsraelWix"), and the notes box is `dir="auto"` (an English note under the Hebrew UI put its full stop first).
- Measured with Playwright, stub model: at 390x664 no page overflows sideways and no control is under 32 px, in both languages; the pages ran 1,790 to 2,185 px, the resume section starting at y = 505-600; the Tracker tab is lit on a job's page (AppLayout's `also`, `document-editor.md`); at 1440 the list sits beside the page and one tap moves between jobs. check-mirrors 65 pins the route, the stretched card link and its `relative` wrappers, no modal, no model call in an effect, the delete handover and its cleared state, and every `also` prefix starting a declared route.
