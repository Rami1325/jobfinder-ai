### A list on a phone, the board from `md` (PLAN 31.2/7, 2026-09-23)

- **What it was, measured at 390x664.** Five counters and two rate rings in one card filled the first screen, and the board's first column started at **y = 609 of 664**. The five status columns were swiped sideways, one per screen, and each card showed its status twice: a split-flap chip and a full-width `<select>` under it.
- **Below `md` it is status tabs over one vertical list** (the list at **y = 290**). Each tab carries its count, and the page opens on the first status that has anything in it, so it never lands on an empty tab while another holds the user's applications. A status change moves the card to its new tab, and C2's accent flash plays on the tab that received it. From `md` the five-column grid is unchanged.
- **Only ONE of the two is mounted** (`useBoardFits`, a live `matchMedia` on 768 px), never both behind CSS. Both render every card, and the board's `layoutId` glide and the split-flap's `pendingFlips` entry each assume one copy of a card on the page: a second copy would register the same `layoutId` and consume the flip the visible one needed.
- **The status is ONE tappable chip**: the split-flap face shows it, a chevron says it opens, and a native `<select>` lies over it, transparent, labelled "Status". A phone opens its own picker and a screen reader hears "Status, Applied". The full-width select is gone, on the board as well.
- **The list's sort includes the date applied** (`lib/trackerSort.ts`): Newest (the day it was added), Date applied, and Match. Under Date applied every row WITH an applied date comes before every row without one, newest first on both sides: an unknown date is not an early one (the house rule), and the explicit rule holds even if the direction is ever flipped. People rely on this sort; LinkedIn's new tracker drew complaints for dropping it, some from people who report their applications for unemployment.
- **The counters are one line** ("3 Total · 1 Applied · 0 Interviews · 0 Offers · 0 Declined"), and **the two rings moved to Analytics**, at its top, with the denominator still riding along as the label ("100%" over one application reads as that). The "gone quiet" nudge comes first, above the counters.
- **A card's controls wrap, and each is a 44 px box** (found in PLAN 31.4/5, fixed 2026-09-27). The card's third row is the date beside a group of two controls, the status chip and "Interviewed". On the board's five columns (from `xl`) a card is 179 px wide, 149 inside its padding, and the two side by side need about 175, while the group could not wrap: "Interviewed" ran 9 px past the card's edge in the Interview column at 1440 and 1 px in Applied (measured). The group now wraps (`flex-wrap min-w-0 justify-end`), so "Interviewed" moves under the status only on a card that narrow, and each control is a `min-h-11` box around its small face (the owner's 44 px touch-target floor; they were 24 and 23 px tall on a phone). The Interviewed toggle also says `aria-pressed`. Measured with Playwright, stub model: nothing past a card's padding at 390 and 360 (the phone list) and at 768, 1024, 1280 and 1440 (the board), in English and Hebrew, no page overflow, both controls 44 px tall everywhere; a card is 139-145 px on a phone (123-129 before, the cost of the two 44 px boxes) and 205 px on the `xl` board in English, where "Interviewed" wraps (161 in Hebrew, where it fits). ~~The stars are still 17 px targets, not changed here.~~ The stars are one 44 px control since the phone polish pass (below). check-mirrors 82 pins the wrap and both boxes.
- **The rating is ONE tappable control, the stars show it** (the phone polish pass, 2026-09-28). A focus ring is drawn on the stars while the hidden select has keyboard focus (`has-[:focus-visible]`). `excitement.set_*` is gone; the words are `excitement.label`, `excitement.none` and `excitement.stars` (plural, Hebrew `_two`).

### One job, one page (PLAN 31.4/2, 2026-09-23)

- **A tracked job has a page of its own, `/applications/:id` (`pages/JobPage.tsx`), and it replaced the detail modal.** It is built from the row and the emails the inbox tied to it (`data-and-privacy.md` has what the row keeps): the header (title, company · place · "Posted …", the status as one tappable chip, the stars, the Match chip only when scored, Open the posting), What they ask for (the stored analysis's must-haves and nice-to-haves, or a sentence saying how to get one, and the posting one tap away), your resume for the job (the draft's REAL first page from `usePageImages`, Word and PDF in the template it was saved in, the way back to it on the document while this tab still holds its review, or Tailor with the fit check's price under it, or a batch draft on its way), the letter (the tailor page's own `CoverLetter`, keyed by the stored analysis so a pass the tailor page opened still covers it; with no analysis stored, "Write a cover letter" reads the posting first, a daily count and never a monthly use, stores it on the row, then shows the card with its price), Prepare (Interview questions, Mock interview, Company brief, Outreach, Follow-up, by their own names, each with `?app=<id>`), the timeline and the notes. From `lg` the applications are a list beside it, mounted only when shown, so a phone never fetches it. **Viewing it never calls a model**: no effect on the page calls a model route (check-mirrors 65), so a model call only follows a tap on a control that states its cost.

### Every finished step offers the next one (PLAN 31.5/6, 2026-09-26)

- A status change ended in silence on the tracker's chip and the job page's chip, and in a View on the document's Mark applied. `hooks/useNextStep` is the one answer, called by all three: **Applied** offers "Remind me if it goes quiet", the reminder emails in the alert form (`/settings#alerts`, which Settings keeps in view while it loads), and **Interview** offers "Practice for this interview", Interview with the job (`/interview?app=<id>`, `useJobContext`, whose Back returns to the job). ### Drafts To review (PLAN 31.4/5, 2026-09-23)

- **A batch's drafts wait on their jobs, in the tracker.** "Tailor my top matches" (the batch card under a search's results) makes one tailored draft per job, a "kit" in the code. They were listed on a Kits tab of the Jobs page, apart from the jobs they were made for, and the count of them rode the Jobs entry. The tab is deleted. The drafts still waiting are **To review**: the FIRST tab of the phone list, shown only while one exists, and a strip over the board from `md` (a strip, not a sixth column: at `xl` six columns leave 168 px a card). The list opens on it while it holds anything, the phone list's rule of the first status with something in it, and a draft's page, its Back and the batch card's "Review them in the tracker" open straight onto it (`{ show: "review" }` in navigation state). Asked for with nothing left, the list opens where it would have.
- **What it lists is `lib/kitsReview.ts`, one answer for the list and the nav.** Ready to review first, then tailoring, queued and failed, newest first within each; never an approved, rejected or submitted draft, which is decided: an approved or sent one IS its job's draft in the tracker now, and a rejected one was turned down. The count on the Tracker entry (`drafts: true` in AppLayout's nav; the search's spinner stays on Jobs, `live`) is the ready ones alone, `awaitingReview`, the status approving requires. A decided draft still blocks a new batch from re-tailoring its job (the server skips any kit that is not `failed`, free); it can be tailored from its job's page instead.
- **A draft card is the size of an application card, and the card IS the way to its page**, the draft's review at `/kits/:id` (its title stretched over the card; the wrapper `relative`, so check 65 counts three such wrappers). Nothing acts in place: approving, rejecting, the letter, the downloads and Delete are on the draft's page, as a job's are on the job's. A match is shown only for a draft that was tailored; a queued one has only the search's reading, a different measurement. The one warning is "N claims to check", and no chip is not a claim that a draft is clean. A batch left queued by a reload continues from the list ("Continue tailoring N queued drafts"), where the Kits tab offered it.
- **The draft's page.** Back leads to To review. An approval or a rejection is put into the kits store (`putKit`): nothing else refreshes the list until a batch finishes, so the nav kept counting a draft just approved. Delete waits out the undo window (check-mirrors 51) and takes the draft object, not its id: the page can be open before the list has loaded, and the old id-only delete returned a no-op then, under a toast saying it was deleted. A load during the window never brings the draft back. The approved and sent banners lead to the job (`/applications/<application_id>`), and the page lights the Tracker tab (`also: ["/applications/", "/kits/"]`).
- **Comeet's send moved to the job's page**, from the Kits tab's rows. It is offered only while the detail names `send_kit` (`data-and-privacy.md`), in a card after the letter, and asks before a REAL application goes; it sends the draft and the letter on the page, then re-reads the job, which is Applied with the send recorded in its notes (the notes box takes that text when nothing was typed in it, or Save would have written the old notes over the record). The company's questionnaire, when it sends one, is a 12-second toast action and stays in the notes. **A send now refuses a draft whose count is not exactly 0**: the kit's flags were read at approval, but a job's draft can be tailored again or typed on after it (31.3/4, 31.4/4), and unknown is never clean.
- **A refused send speaks the reader's language** (found here, fixed 2026-09-27). The refusal toast was the server's English sentence on the Hebrew page too. Every refusal is now `auto_submit.SubmitRefused`, one of the 16 codes in `REFUSAL_CODES` (from `already_sent` and `draft_changed` to Comeet's `comeet_locked`, `comeet_declined` and `comeet_unreachable`, plus the route's `kit_not_found` 404), and the route answers `{detail, code, params}`: `detail` STAYS the English string, because a tab loaded before the codes existed prints a string detail as it is and would have nothing useful to print for an object; `params` carries what a sentence names (`company`, `status`), never Comeet's own words, which stay in the English only. `lib/sendRefusal.ts` maps a code to `job.send.refused.<code>` in `tracker.json`, looked up by the table's own keys (`constructor` is not a code), and an unknown code, from a server newer than the tab, reads the generic `job.send.error`; anything that is not a refusal (the daily cap's `daily_limit`, a session that ended) still goes through `apiErrorMessage`, which translated those already. Driven with the request intercepted in the browser at 390 and 360 in both languages: the reCAPTCHA, decline and changed-draft sentences each named in the page's language ("Lumen Payments מגנה על המשרה הזאת בבדיקת בוטים…"), an unknown code the generic sentence, the daily cap its own sentence, and no toast or page overflow.
